Hermes Agent

A private, single-user automation assistant.

What this application is

Hermes Agent is a personal automation assistant that runs on the owner's own computer. It is used by exactly one person — its owner — to keep their own work organised: reading and answering their own email, keeping their own calendar and task list up to date, and storing documents and spreadsheets they create for their own use.

The application is not offered as a service to the public. There are no other users, no customers, no advertising and no commercial purpose. It does not create accounts for third parties and does not act on behalf of anyone other than its owner.

What it does with Google data

With the owner's permission, and only for the owner's own Google Account, Hermes Agent connects to the following Google services:

  • Gmail — read the owner's mailbox, detect messages that need an answer, and send or draft replies on the owner's behalf when the owner asks for it.
  • Google Calendar — read the owner's agenda and create or update appointments for the owner.
  • Google Drive — store and retrieve documents and spreadsheets the owner produces.
  • Google Docs and Sheets — create and edit the owner's documents and spreadsheets.
  • Google Tasks — record and update the owner's pending tasks and reminders.
  • Google Contacts (read-only) — recognise who is writing, so replies can be addressed correctly.

How the data is handled

  • Data is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • It is never sold, rented, published or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising.
  • Google user data is not transferred to anyone, and is not used to train any model or to build profiles.
  • No human other than the owner reads the data.
